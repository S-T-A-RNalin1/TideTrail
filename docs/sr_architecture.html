Inputs

Imagery

  • Sentinel-2 L2A, 10 m: blue, green, red, NIR
  • Reference 0.6 m aerial (NAIP), for training and checking
  • Upload any georeferenced GeoTIFF

Pre-process

Make pairs match

  • Reflectance, band order
  • Registration by phase correlation
  • Radiometric match to 10 m
  • Cloud screening, quality filter
  • Split by region

Model

Residual CNN, 4x

  • Bicubic base plus learned detail
  • Sub-pixel convolution
  • Predicts the reflectance and its uncertainty
  • Trained on paired scenes

Post-process

Stay faithful

  • Tiled inference, no seams
  • Consistency step: averages back to the measured 10 m
  • 2.5 m GeoTIFF on the exact 4x grid

Validate

Against references

  • Held-out regions, 2.5 m aerial
  • PSNR, SSIM, spectral angle
  • NDVI, water edges, edges
  • Uncertainty checked against real error

Output

Console and API

  • Swipe compare, 10 m and 2.5 m
  • Uncertainty overlay
  • Two GeoTIFFs, REST API
